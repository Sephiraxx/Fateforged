# Intermediate candidates

These aggregates were captured while tuning v10. They are **not final-patch evidence** and their settings differ from the final engine fingerprint in `BALANCE-V10.md`. Earlier captures predate fingerprint metadata.

* `generation-initial.json`: proposed Legendary/Mythic boosts 5.0/6.5, before restoring them to 5.6/7.2.
* `powers-initial.json`: combined candidate with Chain lightning pulse 0.42.
* `chain-030.json`: targeted 0.30 pulse trial; `chain.json`: subsequent targeted 0.26 trial before final tracking refinement.
* `styles-initial.json`, `weapons-initial.json`: before continued melee forward pressure during attack cycles.
* `styles-pressure-065.json`: forward pressure with 0.65 recovery movement, before the final exact 65% tracking boundary.
* `weapons-pressure.json`: intermediate full forward-pressure trial.

The final `generation.json`, `powers.json`, `weapons.json`, `styles.json`, `history.json` and `worlds.json` reside in the parent folder. `careers.json` is a synthetic standings stress check, not a combat-population experiment. The full historical backup and decoded audit remain outside the repository.
