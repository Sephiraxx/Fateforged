Format help

Larger weights give larger slices and higher odds. Ability wheels use Magic for spells and Combat mastery for techniques. Class specialties guide the selection, and equipment must suit the technique. Weights choose within the available themes and rarities. Empty ability slots always give zero stats. A name alone preserves its existing scores, or starts at zero for a new option. Changes apply to this session. Editing a dependent wheel changes the options for its current race or class.

    How scoring works

    Every revealed result adds its five listed stats. Weaknesses subtract points; each final stat bottoms out at zero. Rerolls replace the old contribution. Both ability wheels favor your class and subclass. Spells use your rolled Magic tier to determine their chance; techniques use Combat mastery, from 10% when Untrained to 99% at Combat omniscience. The second ability is 20% less likely and cannot repeat the first. No magic gives 0%, Dormant spark 10%, Apprentice 20%, Novice 30%, Adept 45%, Expert 60%, Master 75%, Archmage 85%, Legendary 92%, Mythic 96%, Reality warper 98%, and Limitless magic 99%. Changing class, specialty, weapon, Magic or Combat mastery clears dependent ability results. These are custom fantasy game scores, including IQ.

    Custom fantasy scoring: stats are game points and never fall below zero.

    Wheel rarity

    Each character’s first Race spin reveals its wheel: Common 50%, Uncommon 25%, Rare 15%, Unique 6%, Legendary 3%, Mythic 1%. Rarity stays with the character until you choose New or Roll whole character. Luckier wheels favor higher-scoring traits and milder stat penalties. Magic controls spell chances; Combat mastery controls technique chances. Rarity adds no stats; all stat values remain those of the rolled traits.

    Editing wheels

    Edit any wheel to change its odds and stat values. Hover a revealed trait or select its wheel to inspect its contribution. Thin slices may hide labels; their options remain in the editor.
